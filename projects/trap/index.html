TRAP

I'm thinking of a number from 1 to 100. You have 6 turns to find it.

Each turn, enter two numbers. They mark off a range, like a trap. I'll tell you if my number is inside your range (trapped), above it, or below it. Landing on either of your two numbers counts as inside.

To win, name my number exactly by entering the same number in both boxes. Trapping it doesn't win by itself. It just tells you where to look.

Example: you enter 20 and 40, and I say "trapped." My number is somewhere from 20 to 40 (inclusive). Next you enter 30 and 30. If my number is 30, you win.
