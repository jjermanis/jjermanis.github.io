ai-trap

Classic number guessing game. Developed with Claude.

Overview

A variant of a "guess a number from 1 to 100" game. Instead of guessing a number using one number, you guess each turn with two numbers.

Directions

For each game, there is a random number between 1 and 100. On each turn you guess with two numbers. For those guesses, you are told if your two numbers are lower than the random number, higher than the random number, or it is between your two guesses. You have six turns to guess the number.

To win the game, both the numbers you guess with must be the same. As an example, consider if the random number is 49. You guess 49 and 50. You will be told that your guess is between the two numbers. You would need to guess 49 and 49 to win.

It is always possible to win every game.

Details

This is a direct adaption of the Trap game from the Basic Computer Games book. The graphics are totally different. The game play is identical.
