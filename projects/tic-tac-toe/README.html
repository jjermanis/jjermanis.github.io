ai-tic-tac-toe

Classic Tic-Tac-Toe against the computer.

Overview

This is standard tic-tac-toe. You will play X. The computer will play O. Mark three of your letters in row, column, or diagonal, and you win that game.

Description

You will consistently play X and go first. The computer will play O. Each game ends in a win, loss, or draw (neither player won). That win-loss-draw record appears at the top of the screen.

Details

The computer knows how to play perfectly. It will not always choose that move. On each turn, 90% of the time, the computer plays the optimal move. 10% of the time, it picks a random open spot. If you play well, you may win about 15% of the time.

I implemented a text-based version of Tic-Tac-Toe in college, freshman year, fall quarter. That version was unkind: you had a 0% chance to win.
