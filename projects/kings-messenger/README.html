kings-messenger

Deliver messages between castles. Developed with ChatGPT.

Overview

You are a messenger. On each level, delivering messages from a village to the castle. There are 4 levels to complete the game.

Directions

On each level you start near the village, toward the left-side of that level. The castle is towards the right. The arrow keys control the direction you move. Hold down the keys to run in that direction. There are no other player controls. They have no weapon.

There different types of terrain. There is water. You cannot go into the water - you need to find a bridge to cross water. There are roads on the ground. These (often) help guide you to the castle. There are trees. You can get close to trees. You cannot go through trees.

There are two types of enemy in the game. Wolves run side-to-side. They do not chase you. If you collide with a wolf, you lose a life. Bandits walk around. If they see you, they will chase you. If you collide with a bandit, you lose a life.

There is a timer at the top of the screen. It counts down. When the timer goes to zero, you lose a life.

You start with three lives. If you lose all lives, the game is over. If you deliver to all four castles you win!

Touch the P key to pause and resume the game.

Details

The game is intended to resemble a Nintendo Entertainment System game from the mid 80's.
